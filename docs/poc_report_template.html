2. Detailed Feature Implementation Deep-Dive Architecture & UI Subsystems
1 Animated Onboarding, Authentication & Persistent JSON Storage
src/pages/auth/ · src/store/useAuthStore.js

Replaces bloated backend setups with an internal JSON-backed state machine. New user signups validate unique mobile/email, issue simulated 4-digit OTP codes, and maintain persistent authenticated sessions across page reloads.

  • SVG brand logo pulse on boot
  • Login / Sign-up layout transition
  • Auto-filling demo accounts for testing
  • Simulated OTP screen with timer resend
2 Dual-Mode Experience Switcher (E-Commerce ↔ Classical Yoga)
src/components/layout/TopPillToggle.jsx · useModeStore.js

A unified single-page architecture that morphs dynamically between two distinct brand personas without changing URL routes or dropping active application state (cart, user, or preferences).

  • aathi:life: Sacred lifestyle marketplace
  • aathi:yoga: Classical asana & studio tutor
  • Smooth ambient background cross-fade
  • Unified bottom navigation & search bar
3 Sacred E-Commerce Catalog & Real-Time Cart CRUD Engine
src/pages/store/ · src/store/useCartStore.js

Covers 9 product categories (Bracelets, Mala, Rings, Rudraksha, Yoga Mats, Accessories, Metal God Idols, Pendants, Tulasi Mala) populated with real scraped assets from aathilife.com.

  • Spring-animated hover pop & scale
  • Price discount badges & stock alerts
  • Add, increment, decrement, and remove
  • Real-time subtotal, 5% GST & checkout
4 Guided Yoga Practice Engine & Circular Timer Player
src/pages/courses/CoursePlayer.jsx · CircularTimer.jsx

A studio practice player for Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced courses. Features interactive countdowns, 3-step posture breakdown with breathing and safety cues, audio gong synthesis, and celebration screens.

  • Circular animated SVG countdown timer
  • Interactive Play / Pause / Skip controls
  • Detailed Step, Breath & Safety instructions
  • Calories burned & practice streak tracker
5 AI Assistant Chatbot with Live Support Fallback
src/components/ChatbotFab.jsx · chatbotRules.js

Floating conversational assistant with organic breathing halo. Answers queries on courses, memberships, pricing, and diet plans with natural typing delays, word-by-word streaming, and smart escalation to support.

  • Organic breathing animation on FAB
  • Natural word-by-word answer streaming
  • One-tap quick suggestion prompt chips
  • Smart fallback button linking to Live Support
